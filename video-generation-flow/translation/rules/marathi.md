Translate into Marathi by applying these rules:

 PRIORITY: When in doubt, prioritize natural, easy-to-understand Marathi over an exact, word-for-word translation. The goal is comprehension, not literal equivalence.

 RULE 1: Colloquial Marathi
   - Marathi words: Use only simple, informal, conversational, day-to-day Marathi (the kind you'd say to friends or type on WhatsApp)
   - English words: Retain specific English words exactly as listed in Rule 2
   - The translation must still be grammatically correct in Marathi

 RULE 2: Keep these words in English (do not translate):
   - Grammar terms and concepts
       Example: We use present continuous tense to talk about actions happening right now → आत्ता होत असलेल्या actions बद्दल बोलायला आपण present continuous tense use करतो
   - Common English phrases, expressions, pleasantries
       Example: Hi!, Perfect!, Example, e.g., Thank you!, For example, Good Job!, Congrats! etc.
   - Proper nouns, brand names, people names, places
       Example: Martin wants to dance → Martin ला dance करायचा आहे
   - English words everyone already uses in Marathi speech (computer, phone, internet, office, bus, train, time, late, please, ready, clean, help, example, correct, mistake, holiday, etc.)
       Example: Could you please help me fix my phone? → तुम्ही please माझा phone fix करण्यासाठी help कराल का?
   - Words where the Marathi equivalent sounds too formal/complex/outdated
       Example: I'm free on the weekends → मी weekends ला free असतो
   - Text that is inside quotes.  Preserve English text inside double (" ") or single (' ') quotes. If the same text appears outside the quotes, translate only the outside one.
       Example: In the sentence "He was strong," what is the linking verb? → "He was strong" या sentence मध्ये linking verb कोणता?

 RULE 3: If the same text appears both inside and outside quotes, keep only the quoted version in English; translate the outside version to Marathi.
   Example 1: To say he is running in English, say "He is running" → इंग्लिशमध्ये तो पळतोय म्हणायचं असेल तर, "He is running" असं म्हण
   Example 2: To say we will be eating in English, say "We will be eating" → इंग्लिशमध्ये आपण खात राहू म्हणायचं असेल तर, "We will be eating" असं म्हण

 RULE 4: Sentence restructuring allowed
   - Feel free to reorder words so the sentence sounds natural in Marathi, rather than copying English structure
     Example: We use this tense to describe ongoing actions. → चालू actions describe करायला आपण हा tense use करतो

 RULE 5: Clarifying additions allowed
   - You may add tiny clarifying words (म्हणजे, तर, खरं तर, कारण, बघ, वगैरे) to make the line sound more natural
     Example 1: Let's learn about Articles! We use articles (a, an, the) before nouns to show whether we're talking about something specific or general. → चला Articles शिकू! Noun च्या आधी articles (a, an, the) use करून आपण specific गोष्ट बोलतोय का general हे दाखवतो
     Example 2: Subject can be: you, we, they. In "They are working": Subject is 'They', Helping verb is 'are', and Verb + ing is 'working' → Subject म्हणजे: you, we, they असू शकतात. "They are working" या sentence मध्ये: Subject आहे 'They', Helping verb आहे 'are', आणि Verb+ing आहे 'working'

 RULE 6: Pronoun translations
   - He → तो
   - She → ती
   - It → हे / ते (context)
   - You → तू
   - They → ते
   - We → आपण
   - I → मी

 RULE 7: Script consistency
   - Keep English words in Roman script and Marathi in Devanagari script. Avoid transliteration of either language.
      ✅ आपण Present Continuous tense use करतो
      ❌ आपण प्रेझेंट कंटिन्युअस टेन्स यूज़ करतो
      ❌ Apan Present Continuous tense use karto

 RULE 8: Tone
   - Maintain a friendly, conversational, teacher-like tone—never robotic or textbook-ish
