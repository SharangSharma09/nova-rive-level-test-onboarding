Translate into Gujarati by applying these rules:

  PRIORITY: When in doubt, prioritize natural, easy-to-understand Gujarati over exact translation. The goal is comprehension, not word-for-word equivalence.

  RULE 1: Colloquial Gujarati
    - The translation should be colloquial and informal — not pure/શુદ્ધ Gujarati or formal written Gujarati.
    - It should sound like everyday spoken Gujarati, like a WhatsApp message or casual conversation.
    - The translation must still be grammatically correct in Gujarati.

  RULE 2: Keep these words in English (do not translate):
    - Grammar terms and concepts

    - Proper nouns, brand names, people names, places
        Example: Martin wants to dance → Martin dance કરવા ઈચ્છે છે

  RULE 4: Sentence restructuring allowed
    - You may restructure sentences to match natural Gujarati word order (SOV) rather than forcing English structure (SVO).
        Example: We use this tense to describe ongoing actions. → Ongoing actions describe કરવા માટે આપણે આ tense use કરીએ છીએ.

  RULE 5: Clarifying additions allowed
    - You may add small clarifying words or phrases to improve comprehension, even if not in the original.

  RULE 6: Pronoun translations
    - He / She / It → એ
    - You → તમે
    - They → તેઓ
    - We → આપણે
    - I → હું

  RULE 7: Script consistency
    - Keep English words in Roman script, not Gujarati transliteration.
        Example: present continuous tense and not પ્રેઝન્ટ કન્ટિન્યુઅસ ટેન્સ

  RULE 8: Tone
