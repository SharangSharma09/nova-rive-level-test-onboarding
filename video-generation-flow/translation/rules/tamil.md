Translate into Tamil by applying these rules:
PRIORITY: When in doubt, prioritize natural, easy-to-understand Tamil over exact translation. The goal is comprehension, not word-for-word equivalence.
RULE 1: Colloquial Tamil
  - The translation should be colloquial and informal — not pure/செந்தமிழ் or formal written Tamil (எழுத்து தமிழ்).
  - It should sound like casual everyday spoken Tamil (பேச்சு தமிழ்), like a WhatsApp message or casual conversation.
  - Use spoken verb forms (e.g., போறேன் instead of போகிறேன், பண்றேன் instead of செய்கிறேன்).
  - The translation must still be grammatically correct in Tamil.
  - Always Use appropriate sentence structure and wordings to maintain natural Tamil conversation flow and informal conversational tone:
RULE 2: Keep these words in English (do not translate):
  - Grammar terms and concepts
      Example: We use present continuous tense to describe about actions happening right now → இப்போ நடந்துகிட்டு இருக்க actions பத்தி describe பண்றதுக்கு present continuous tense அ நாம use பண்றோம்
  - Common English phrases, expressions, pleasantries
      Example: Hi!, Perfect!, Example, e.g., Thank you!, For example, Good Job!, Congrats! etc.
  - Proper nouns, brand names, people names, places
      Example 1: Martin wants to dance → Martin dance ஆட ஆசப்படுறான்
      Example 2: Bella will be cleaning the room → Bella room ஐ clean பண்ணிட்டு இருப்பாள்
  - English words commonly used in spoken Tamil without Tamil equivalents (e.g., computer, phone, internet, office, bus, train)
      Example: Could you please help me fix my phone? → Please என் phoneஅ fix பண்ண help பண்ண முடியுமா?
  - Words where the Tamil equivalent sounds too formal, complex, or outdated
      Example 1: I'm free on the weekends → நான் weekends ல free ஆ இருப்பேன் (not நான் வார இறுதியில் ஓய்வாக இருப்பேன்)
      Example 2: I have a meeting now → எனக்கு இப்போ meeting இருக்கு (not எனக்கு இப்போது கூட்டம் உள்ளது)
  - When retaining the english words while translating, Use the appropriate Tamil connector words such as ஐ, க்கு, ல, ஒட/ஓட, அ, உம், மேல, கிட்ட, கூட, காக and any others that fit the context and apply them accordingly without adding a dash, so the English Tamil mixed sentences flow naturally and sound smooth.
      Example 1: I have already booked the tickets for the show -> நான் show க்கான tickets ஐ already book பண்ணிட்டேன்
      Example 2: There is a problem in my friend's car -> என் Friend ஓட car ல ஒரு problem இருக்கு
      Example 3: I have to pay for the loan -> நான் loan க்கு pay பண்ணணும்
RULE 3: Do not translate text inside double quotes
  - Only preserve English text that is *explicitly enclosed in double quotes* (" ").
  - The same text appearing *outside* of quotes should still be translated normally.
  - Double quotes are used to highlight example sentences being taught — only those should remain in English.
      Example 1: In the sentence "He is running," the verb shows continuous action. → "He is running" இந்த sentence ல, verb continuous action ஐ காட்டுது.
      Example 2: In the sentence "He was strong," "He" or "was", what is called as the linking verb ? → "He was strong" இந்த sentence ல, "He" அல்லது "Was" ல, எத linking verb னு சொல்லுவோம்?
      Example 3: To say I exercise everyday in English, say "I exercise everyday" → English ல நான் தினமும் exercise பண்றேன்னு சொல்ல, "I exercise everyday"னு சொல்லுங்க
RULE 4: Clarifying additions allowed
  - You may modify or add small clarifying words or phrases (like தான், ங்குறது, அதுனால, இல்ல, என்னனா, மட்டும், அப்பறம், அன்னிக்கு, அப்போ, அதுக்கு, வரைக்கும், முன்னாடி, லேர்ந்து, etc) to improve comprehension without altering the meaning.      
      Example 1: On march 30th, we lost the game only because of that mistake." → March 30th அன்னிக்கு அந்த mistake னால மட்டும் தான் நாம game ஐ lose பண்ணிட்டோம்
      Example 2: Subject can be: you, we, they. In "They are working" : Subject is 'They', Helping verb is 'are', and Verb + ing is 'working' → இங்க you, we, they லாம் Subject ஆக இருக்கலாம். அதாவுது "They are working" ங்குறதுல: Subject வந்து 'They', Helping verb வந்து 'are', Verb + ing தான் 'working'.
RULE 5: Sentence restructuring allowed
  - Reorder words and restructure sentences to match natural Tamil word order (SOV) and flow, rather than forcing English structure (SVO).
  - Time expressions often come first in Tamil sentences.
  - Conditional and temporal clauses ("when," "while," "if") should come before the main clause.
  - Purpose clauses ("so that," "in order to") naturally flow before or integrated into the main action.
      Example 1: Now let's practice so you don't forget this. → இப்போ நீங்க இத மறக்காம இருக்கத்துக்காக நாம practice பண்ணலாம்.
      Example 2: We use this tense when we want to say an action happened in the past. → எதாவது ஒரு action past ல நடந்துச்சுன்னு சொல்லணும்னா இந்த tense அ நாம use பண்றோம்.
      Example 3: They spoke quietly while the baby slept → Baby தூங்கிட்டு இருந்தப்போ அவங்க மெதுவா பேசுனாங்க
      Example 4: I will go to the store after I finish my work → நான் என் work முடிச்சதுக்கு அப்பறம் store க்கு போவேன்
RULE 6: Script consistency
  - Keep English words in Roman script, not Tamil transliteration.
      ✅ present continuous tense
      ❌ பிரசன்ட் கன்டினியஸ் டென்ஸ்
RULE 7: Pronoun usage for objects and non-human subjects
  - For objects, concepts, and non-human things, use அது/அத (it), இத/இந்த/இதுலாம் (this/these) — not respectful forms like இவங்க/அவங்க which is used only for people or groups of people.
      Example: These are slightly tricky verbs. → இந்த verbs லாம் கொஞ்சம் சிக்கலானது.
RULE 8: Tone
  - Maintain a friendly, conversational, teacher-like tone — not robotic or textbook-ish.
