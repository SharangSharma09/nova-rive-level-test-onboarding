Translate into Bengali by applying these rules:

  PRIORITY: When in doubt, prioritize natural, easy-to-understand Bengali over an exact, word-for-word translation. The goal is comprehension, not literal equivalence.

  RULE 1: Colloquial Bengali
	  - Bengali words: Use only simple, informal, conversational, day-to-day Bangla (the kind you'd say to friends or type on WhatsApp)
	  - English words: Retain specific English words exactly as listed in Rule 2
	  - The translation must still be grammatically correct in Bengali

  RULE 2: Keep these words in English (do not translate):
	  - Grammar terms and concepts
	    Example: We use present continuous tense to talk about actions happening right now → এখন যে actions হচ্ছে সেগুলো বলতে আমরা present continuous tense use করি
	  - Common English phrases, expressions, pleasantries
	    Example: Hi!, Perfect!, Example, e.g., Thank you!, For example, Good Job!, Congrats! etc.
	  - Proper nouns, brand names, people names, places
	    Example: Martin wants to dance → Martin dance করতে চায়
	  - English words everyone already uses in Bangla speech (computer, phone, internet, office, bus, train, time, late, please, ready, clean, help, example, correct, mistake, holiday, etc)
	    Example: Could you please help me fix my phone? → তুমি please আমার phone টা fix করতে help করবে?
	  - Words where the Bengali equivalent sounds too formal/complex/outdated
      Example 1: I'm free on the weekends → আমি weekends এ free থাকি (instead of আমি সপ্তাহান্তে অবসর থাকি)
	  - Text that is inside quotes. Preserve English text inside double (" ") or single (' ') quotes. If the same text appears outside the quotes, translate only the outside one.
	    Example: In the sentence "He was strong," what is the linking verb? → "He was strong" এই sentence এ linking verb কোনটা?

  RULE 3: If the same text appears both inside and outside quotes, keep only the quoted version in English; translate the outside version to Bengali.
	  Example 1: To say he is running in English, say "He is running" → ইংরেজিতে সে দৌড়াচ্ছে বলার জন্য, "He is running" বলো
    Example 2: To say we will be eating in English, say "We will be eating" → ইংরেজিতে আমরা খেতে থাকবো বলার জন্য, "We will be eating" বলো

  RULE 4: Sentence restructuring allowed
	  - Feel free to reorder words so the sentence sounds natural in Bengali, rather than copying English structure
	    Example: We use this tense to describe ongoing actions. → চলমান কাজ describe করতে আমরা এই tense use করি

  RULE 5: Clarifying additions allowed
	  - You may add tiny clarifying words (মানে, তো, আসলে, যেন, কারণ, ঠিক, ইত্যাদি) to make the line sound more natural
	    Example 1: Let's learn about Articles! We use articles (a, an, the) before nouns to show whether we're talking about something specific or general. → চলো Articles সম্পর্কে শিখি! Noun এর আগে articles (a, an, the) use করে আমরা বুঝিয়ে দিই আমরা কি specific কিছু নিয়ে বলছি নাকি general ভাবে বলছি
      Example 2: Subject can be: you, we, they. In "They are working" : Subject is 'They', Helping verb is 'are', and Verb + ing is 'working' → Subject you, we, they হতে পারে. "They are working" sentence এ: Subject হল 'They', Helping verb হল 'are', এবং Verb + ing হল 'working'

  RULE 6: Pronoun translations
    - He(ছেলেটি) / She(মেয়েটি) → সে
    - It  → এটা / ওটা (pick based on context)
	  - You → তুমি
	  - They → ওরা
	  - We  → আমরা
	  - I   → আমি

  RULE 7: Script consistency
	  - Keep English words in Roman script, not Bengali transliteration
      ✅ present continuous tense
      ❌ প্রেজেন্ট কন্টিনিউয়াস টেন্স

  RULE 8: Tone
	  - Maintain a friendly, conversational, teacher-like tone—never robotic or textbook-ish
