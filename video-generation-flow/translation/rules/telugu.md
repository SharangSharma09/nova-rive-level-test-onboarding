Translate into Telugu by applying these rules:

  PRIORITY: When in doubt, prioritize natural, easy-to-understand Telugu over exact translation. The goal is comprehension, not word-for-word equivalence.

  RULE 1: Colloquial Telugu
	  - Telugu words: Use only simple, informal, conversational and colloquial Telugu used in everyday speech
	  - English words: Retain specific English words as listed in Rule 2
	  - The translation should always be grammatically correct in Telugu.
	
  RULE 2: Keep these words in English (do not translate):
    - Grammar terms and concepts
      Example: We use present continuous tense to talk about actions happening right now →  మనము ఇప్పుడు జరుగుతున్న actions గురించి మాట్లాడడానికి Present Continuous Tense use చేస్తాము 
    - Common English phrases, expressions, pleasantries
      Example: Hi!, Perfect!, Example, e.g., Thank you!, For example, Good Job!, Congrats! etc.
    - Proper nouns, brand names, people names, places
      Example: Martin wants to dance → Martin dance చేయాలనుకుంటున్నాడు
    - English words commonly used in spoken Telugu without Telugu equivalents (e.g., computer, phone, internet, office, bus, train)
      Example: Could you please help me fix my phone? → Please, నా phone fix చేయడంలో నాకు help చేయగలరా?
    - Words where the Telugu equivalent sounds too formal, complex, or outdated
      Example 1: I'm free on the weekends → నేను weekends లో free గా ఉంటాను instead of నేను వారాంతాల్లో ఖాళీగా ఉంటాను
      Example 2: Because we are describing here, we use Future continuous tense → क्योंकि यहाँ हम describe कर रहे हैं, इसलिए हम Future continuous tense use करते हैं (not यहाँ हम वर्णन कर रहे हैं, अतः हम Future continuous tense उपयोग करते हैं.)
    - Text that is inside quotes. Preserve English text that is explicitly enclosed in double quotes (" ") or single quotes (' '). The same text can be outside quotes and needs to be translated to Telugu.
      Example 1: In the sentence "He is running," the verb shows continuous action. → "He is running" ఈ sentence లో, verb continuous action చూపిస్తుంది.
      Example 2: In the sentence "He was strong," what is the linking verb? → "He was strong" ఈ sentence లో linking verb ఏమిటి??

  RULE 3: If the same text appears both inside and outside quotes, only keep the quoted one in English.
    - Example: To say he is running in English, say "He is running" → English లో అతను పరగేతు తున్నాడు అని చప్పటానికి, "He is running" అని చెప్పండి.

  RULE 4: Sentence restructuring allowed
    - You may restructure sentences to match natural Telugu word order rather than forcing English structure.
      Example: We use this tense to describe ongoing actions. → Ongoing actions describe చేయడానికి మనం ఈ tense use చేస్తాము.

  RULE 7: Script consistency
	  - Keep English words in Roman script, not Telugu transliteration.
    Example: present continuous tense and not ప్రెజెంట్ కాంటిన్యూయస్ టెన్స్
